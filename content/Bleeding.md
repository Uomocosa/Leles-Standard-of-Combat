You loose **1d4** [[Health]] at the end of every [[Phases of Combat|Turn]].
If a Bleeding Creature gets [[Heal and Repair|Healed]] for any reason, the **Bleeding** ends.
A [[Medicine]] [[Skill Check|check]] with [[Skill Checks|DC]] **15** can stop the **Bleeding**.

The Bleeding Condition can be given by the **DM**. Usually it is given after a strong blow that causes a Creature to loose a lot of [[Health]], or to signal to the Players that a Creature is verging on [[Dying and Stable|Dying]].
