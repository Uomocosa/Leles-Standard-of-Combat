- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>When Creating a Scroll or Potion, you first need to decide what kind of Spell you want to Imbue in it.<br>The Spell you Imbue follows the same rules as any other Spell you Create, however you don't Cast it, so you don't need to expend [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]].<br><br>To Create a Scroll or Potion you must pay **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Point]]** equal to **half the number of Runes used in the Spell** (_rounded up_).<br>You also need to spend **1 hour** imbuing the Spell, and succeed an [[SoC • Rule - Anima|Anima]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|check]] with **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] equal to 10 + 3 times the number of Runes used in the Spell**. (_failing does not consume **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**_)

- ***Rarity***: uncommon
- ***Color***: artifact, multicolor
