During [[Phases of Combat|Combat]] at the beginning of every [[Phases of Combat|Round]], all Creatures involved prepare (_usually_) **2 Actions**.
These prepared actions represent the intention/s that each Creature has for that **Round**.

You can also pay **1 [[SoC • Energy|Energy]]** to exchange one of your [[Prepare to Act|Prepared Actions]], with another one.
**Prepared Action** can be used as [[Reactions]].
