- ***Type***: Optional Rule
- ***Description***:<br>_Optional Rule_ — Your **GM** will decide if they want to add this rule to the game.<br><br>The first time your **Party** does something cool (_and not too far fetched_), reward them by make it succeed without requiring any [[SoC • Rules - Skill Checks|Skill Check]].<br>After that the next time/s they make it again roll [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Checks]] as normal.<br>If they did a cool Action, but really powerful, the next time make sure it is at least difficult to do, give them **2 or more [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantages]]** or make the **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] really high (25 or more)**.<br><br>_As a Player it should feel rewarding using your imagination, and coming up with clever solutions every time._<br>_As a **GM** try not to let them to use only use a single tactic or broken combo, for all the Campaign._

- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Color***: artifact, white
