- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>A **d20** roll of **20** scores a **Critical Success**, and a **1** a **Critical Failure**.<br>Each of these rolls will create a maximal version of success or failure.<br><br>_Critical Hit_ — If you roll a Critical Success to hit, and you would roll one ore more dices to deal damage, roll all of them twice and sum the results.
