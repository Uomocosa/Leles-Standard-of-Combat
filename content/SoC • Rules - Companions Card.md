- ***Name***: Companions
- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>**Starting Cost** — You need to spend **5 pts** to get a companion. Like you it starts with **3 [[Health]]** and **2 [[Energy|Energy]]**.<br>**Second Character** — Your Companion is basically a second Character (_or pet_), that you build from scratch, and customize, using your **[[Hero Points]]**. Consider however that a Companion is not entirely yours, both you and your GM can decide what actions he/she will take, with the **GM**, as always, having the final say.<br>**Fewer are Better** — Don't create too many companions, so that your **GM** is not forced to kill them off. ***Let the other players can have their turn too!***<br>**Death of a Companion** — When a companion is killed your **GM** decides if, and how many [[Hero Points]] you get back.
