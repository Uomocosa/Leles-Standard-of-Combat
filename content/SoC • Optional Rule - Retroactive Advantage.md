- ***Type***: Optional Rule
- ***Description***:<br>_Optional Rule_ — Your **GM** will decide if they want to add this rule to the game.<br><br>_Your players may ask to perform Reactions **after** seeing a failed roll — and that’s fair! But fully re-rolling a failed attempt can be too powerful and game-breaking_.<br>Instead, **pause** before narrating the outcome. If a player has a Spell or wants to attempt a desperate [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] to help (_e.g., giving Advantage to an Ally's Roll_), adjust the result as follows:<br>• If the original roll had one or more **Disadvantages**, take the **second-lowest** die instead of the lowest.<br>• If it had **no Advantages**, roll **one additional d20** and use that result instead.
