- ***Type***: Optional Rule
- ***Description***:<br>_Optional Rule_ — Your **GM** will decide if they want to add this rule to the game.<br><br>Remove the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Actions]]: "**[[SoC • Action - Attack|Attack]]**", "**[[SoC • Action - Use an Item|Use an Item]]**", and "**[[SoC • Action - Cast a Spell|Cast a Spell]]**".<br><br>_Preparing to Attack/Use an Item_ — Place down as a **[[SoC • Rules - Prepare to Act|Prepared Action]]**, the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - BASIC EQUIPMENT|Weapon]], or [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ITEMS|Item]], you want to use.<br><br>If you didn't already do it, when Learning a **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Rune]]** add to your Deck its [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Rune Card]].<br>_Preparing a **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spell]]**_ — Place a small pile of **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Rune Cards]]** face-down as your **[[SoC • Rules - Prepare to Act|Prepared Action]]** (_at most **5 Rune Cards**_).<br>_Casting a **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spell]]**_ — You **must combine all Runes you placed down** into a Spell. You may include **multiple copies** of each Rune, but you must use **at least one copy of every Rune** in the pile when casting.
- ***Flavor***: _This limits a bit the power of Casting Spells during [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Combat]], which could be nice!_
