- ***Type***: Optional Rule
- ***Description***:<br>_Optional Rule_ — Your **GM** will decide if they want to add this rule to the game.<br><br>**Remove the [[SoC • Rune - Dispel|"Dispel" Rune]]**. <br>**Rune Effects can now Overload** — When two Effects of the same Rune type are applied to the same target by different Spells, **both Effects are Dispelled**.<br>_Identifying which Rune Effects are active in a Spell becomes essential_. You can use your [[Perception]], or [[Intuition]] to analyze a Spell.<br><br>_Example_ — To **Counter** a Spell using the **[[SoC • Rune - Harm|Rune of Harm]]**, you can use a [[Reactions|Reaction]] to target the same Creature and apply another Harming Effect, like _"Your punches deal **1d4** damages"_. When the original Spell lands, both Harm effects **Overload**, and **both Spells are immediately Dispelled**.
