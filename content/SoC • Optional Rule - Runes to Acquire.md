- ***Type***: Optional Rule
- ***Description***:<br>_Optional Rule_ — Your **GM** will decide if they want to add this rule to the game.<br><br>Each Character starts with a chosen or random **Rune** (_a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spell Effect]]_).<br>Every time a Character reaches **+5** in a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill]], it gains access to another **Rune** of its choice.<br>A Character can only add [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spell Effects]] and Create new [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spells]] using its **Runes**, your **allies** can help you while creating and modifying Spells by adding their **Runes**.<br>Remove or change the "[[SoC • Core Ability - Runic Caster|Runic Caster]]" [[SoC • Rules - Core Ability|Core Ability]].<br>The "[[SoC • Rule - Passive Ability|Passive]]" [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Effect]] cannot become a **Rune**, to make a Spell that cost **2 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]] or less** into a Passive, a Character needs to pay **3 pts**.<br>The "[[SoC • Rule - Lasting Spells|Lasting]]" [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Effect]] cannot become a **Rune**, to make a Spell that cost **1 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]]** into a Lasting Spell, a Character needs to pay **1 pt**.
