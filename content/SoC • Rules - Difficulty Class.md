- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>When performing an action that requires a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] and **is not** an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] against another Creature, your GM will set a **Difficulty Class (DC)** for the task.<br>If your total equals or exceeds the DC, you succeed. If it falls short, you fail.<br><br>_Here are some examples_:<br>• **Simple Action** — Jumping a small gap without tripping, while being chased. (_DC **5** on [[Agility]] or [[Might]]_)<br>• **Difficult Action** — Move without making any noise. (_DC **15** on [[Stealth]]_)<br>• **Almost Impossible Action** — Raising a massive, magically reinforced stone or metal gate without assistance. (_DC **45** on [[Might]]_)
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Color***: artifact, multicolor
