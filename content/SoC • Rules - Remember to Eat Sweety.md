- ***Name***: Remember to Eat, Sweety
- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>Each day you need to consume at least one count of [[SoC • Item - Rations|Rations]].<br>• If you don't eat or drink anything, for **1 day**: you are **[[Slowed]]** (_[[SoC • Generic Action - Movement or Skill|Moving]] becomes more strenuous_) until you eat again.<br>• If you don't eat or drink anything, for **2 days straight**: you don't gain any benefits from [[Rest and Recovery|Resting]] (_neither from [[Rest and Recovery|a Night of Rest]], nor from a [[Rest and Recovery|Short Rest]]_), until you eat again.<br>• If you don't eat or drink anything, for **more than 2 days straight**: gain a [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]] each day after the second (_when you have **3** you [[Death|Die]]_).
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Color***: artifact, white
