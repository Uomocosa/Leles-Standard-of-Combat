Those of you who failed the Perception check are surprised and cannot act during this round."_<br><br>A **Surprise Round** occurs when one or more creatures are caught off guard at the start of combat. It represents a tactical advantage gained through stealth, deception, or ambush.<br><br>Usually it goes something like this:<br>1. A creature or group must remain hidden or unnoticed by their targets. This often requires a successful [[Stealth]] [[Skill Checks|check]].<br>2. The targets make [[Perception]] [[Skill Checks|check]] to detect the ambush. If they fail to notice the threat, they are surprised.<br>3. If one or more creature succeeds in being unnoticed, then they will act in an additional round before combat begins (the **Surprise Round**), afterwards combat goes as usual.
