- ***Type***: Introduction
- ***Description***:<br>As a **GM** you can use **Zone Cards**, to define where the combat takes place.<br><br>If two cards touch each other, then a Creature can move between them.<br>To define the distance between two Creatures:<br>• ***Same Zone***<br>• ***No Zone in between / in adjacent Zones***<br>• ***One Zone in between***.<br>• ***Two Zones in between***.<br>• ...
- ***Flavor***: You can also use a simple drawing instead of Zone Cards.
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Color***: artifact, multicolor
