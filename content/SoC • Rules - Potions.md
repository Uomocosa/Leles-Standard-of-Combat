- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>You can create a Potion by imbuing it with a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spell]] you know, or by crafting a temporary [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Custom Spell]] using your available [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spells]] and [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Runes]]. When doing so, you only pay **half its [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Point]] cost** (_rounded up_).<br>You also need to spend **1 hour** imbuing the spell, and succeed an [[SoC • Rule - Anima|Anima]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] with **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] equal to 10 + 3 times the cost of the spell**. (_failing does not consume **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**_)<br><br>Like a Scroll, a spell casted from a Potion requiring one or more [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Checks]] uses the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Modifiers]] of the Creature that made it. And after the spell imbued in a Potion is casted the item is [[SoC • Rules - Destroyed Gear|Destroyed]].<br><br>A Potion does not require to be deciphered to be used, you can drink it, or throw it, and the Spell's Effects are applied on the Creature or [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]] it falls on.<br>To throw a Potion at a Creature, make a **[[SoC • Rules - Melee and Ranged Attacks|Ranged Attack]]** against it.<br><br>You can still try do decipher it first, but it will be very difficult, you need to spend **1 hour** studying it, and succeed an [[Intuition]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|check]] with **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] equal to 10 + 5 times the cost of the spell***.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Rarity***: uncommon
