- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>You can create a Potion by imbuing a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spell]] you know, that does not have a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Limitation]], and pay half its cost in [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]] (_rounded up_).<br>You also need to spend **1 hour** imbuing the spell, and succeed an [[Arcane]], [[Nature]] or [[Faith]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|check]] with [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] equal to **10 + 3 times the cost of the spell**.<br><br>Like a Scroll, a spell casted from a Potion requiring one or more [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Checks]] uses the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Modifiers]] of the Creature that made it. And after the spell imbued in a Potion is casted the item is [[SoC • Rules - Destroyed Gear|Destroyed]].<br><br>A potion does not require to be deciphered to be used, you can drink it, or throw it, and the spell effects are applied on the Creature or [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]] it falls on.<br>You can throw it in your [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]] or a nearby one without difficulty. If you wish to target a Creature with the potion you need to succeed in a [[SoC • Rules - Melee and Ranged Attacks|Ranged Attack]] against it.<br><br>You can still try do decipher it first, but it will be very difficult, you need to spend **1 hour** studying it, and succeed an [[Intuition]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|check]] with [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] equal to **10 + 5 times the cost of the spell***.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Rarity***: uncommon
- ***Color***: artifact, blue, red
