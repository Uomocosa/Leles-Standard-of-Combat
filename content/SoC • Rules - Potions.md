- ***Description***:<br>Like a Scroll, a spell casted from a Potion requiring one or more [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Checks]] uses the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Modifiers]] of the Creature that made it. And after the spell imbued in a Potion is casted the item is [[SoC • Rules - Destroyed Gear|Destroyed]]. (_You don't need to pay any [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]]_.) <br><br>A Potion does not require to be deciphered to be used, you can drink it, or throw it, and the Spell's Effects are applied on the Creature or [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]] it falls on.<br>To throw a Potion at a Creature, make a **[[SoC • Rules - Melee and Ranged Attacks|Ranged Attack]]** against it.<br><br>You can still try do decipher it first, but it will be very difficult, you need to spend **1 hour** studying it, and succeed an [[Intuition]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|check]] with **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] equal to 10 + 5 times the cost of the spell**.
