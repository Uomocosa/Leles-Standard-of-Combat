- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>If you’re willing, other Creatures in your same **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]]** may aide you in your Action. Each helper must [[SoC • Rules - Forgo an Action|Forgo an Action]] to help you.<br><br>_Casting a Spell_ — If you are Casting a Spell they may contribute their own [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Runes]] to Modify the Spell’s Effect. For each Rune they add, they must pay **1 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]]**. As with any [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spell]], it can include up to **5 Runes** in total.<br>_Attacking_ — If you are Attacking they may cast a Spell before your Attack reaches his Target, or similar. Or they may use their Action and try do distract the Target, giving you [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on your Roll.<br><br>Helping others is a **[[Reactions|Reaction]]**, it can be done only during the **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Turn]] of a willing Creature**, and does **not** require any **additional [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]]**.

- ***Rarity***: common
