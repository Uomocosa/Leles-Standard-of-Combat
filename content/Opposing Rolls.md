An opposing roll is made between two or more Creatures.
Each Creature involved is either **the attacker** or **a defender**. 
If a defender rolls **higher** than the attacker then, _usually_, nothing happens to the defender. (_If the two rolls are **equal**, than the attacker wins the roll_)

Here's the most common examples of _opposing rolls_:
- _Melee Attack_ — Succeed in an [[Opposing Rolls|opposing roll]]: _[[Might]] **vs.** [[Might]] or [[Agility]]_ (_defender uses lowest_) to hit.
- _Ranged Attack_ — Succeed in an [[Opposing Rolls|opposing roll]]: _[[Agility]] **vs.** [[Agility]]_ to hit.
- _Spell Effect_ — Succeed in an [[Opposing Rolls|opposing roll]]: _[[Arcane]], [[Faith]] or [[Nature]] **vs.** [[Might]] or [[Agility]]_ (_Choose which [[SoC • Generic Card - Character Details|Skills]] are used when you add this effect_) to apply the spell's effects.
- _Spell Effect II_ — Succeed in an [[Opposing Rolls|opposing roll]]: _[[Arcane]], [[Faith]] or [[Nature]] **vs.** [[Perception]]_ (_Choose which [[SoC • Generic Card - Character Details|Skills]] to use when you add this effect_)
- _Intimidation_ — Each Creature affected makes an [[Opposing Rolls|opposing roll]] on [[Will]] against your [[Intimidation]] [[Skill Check]], those that fail are [[Scared]] of you for **1 [[Phases of Combat|Turn]]** (_must flee if in the same Zone_).
