- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>Like a Potion, a Spell casted from a Scroll requiring one or more [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Checks]] **uses the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Modifiers]] of the Creature that made it**. And after the spell imbued in a Scroll is casted the item is **[[SoC • Rules - Destroyed Gear|Destroyed]]**. (_You don't need to pay any [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]]_.) <br><br>An unknown scroll can be difficult to read, but since you made it you can easily teach it your allies, so that they can use it as well.<br>To decipher an unknown scroll, you need to spend **1 hour** studying it, and succeed an [[Intuition]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|check]] with **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] equal to 10 + 3 times the cost of the spell**.<br><br>If you read the scroll out-loud you simply cast the Spell.<br>Also, you can imbue the Scroll with [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]] and lay it on a surface, when a Creature touches it, the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spell]] gets Casted.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Rarity***: uncommon
- ***Color***: artifact, blue, white
