- ***Info***: Condition
- ***Description***:<br>You cannot see. As long as you don’t know where a Creature is, you cannot target it with [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|Attacks]] or [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spells]].<br>You can still attempt to perceive your surroundings using your other senses, but this requires a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] (_your **GM** will determine the exact details_).<br><br>By default, if you are Blinded, you may try to sense what’s happening around it. Roll a [[Perception]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]]:<br>• On a **15 or higher**, you act with [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on all [[SoC • Rules - Skill Checks|Skill Checks]] that target other Creatures, during this [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Round]].<br>• On a **20 or higher**, you somehow perceive everything clearly, **no penalty is applied**.
