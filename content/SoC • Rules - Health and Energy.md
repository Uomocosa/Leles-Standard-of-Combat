- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>**Health** — When you loose health (_for example by receiving damage_): you [[SoC • Rules - Discard Pile|discard]] that number of Health Cards.<br>You regain Health by resting, or if healed by a spell effect.<br>If you have no more Health Cards you start Dying, so be careful!<br><br>**Energy** — Whenever you need to pay Energy: you [[SoC • Rules - Discard Pile|discard]] that number of Energy Cards.<br>Energy is necessary to cast [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spells]], use one of your [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Prepared Action]] as a [[Reactions]] during another Creature's [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Turn]], exchange one of your [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Prepared Action]] with another one, ...
- ***Flavor***: **Energy** can be interpreted by each player as their own unique resource: such as Mana, Prana, Aura, Ki, Chi, or even something as simple as an _"I'm-not-too-tired-yet"_ kind of feeling.
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Color***: artifact, white, blue
