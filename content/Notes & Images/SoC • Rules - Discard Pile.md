- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>Your Discard Pile is for all the cards that where **Discarded** (_**consumed** or expended_), and require [[SoC • Rules - A Night of Rest|A Night of Rest]], [[Heal and Repair|Repair]], or similar to be regained.<br>For example when you lose [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health]], [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]], or a piece of [[SoC • Rules Chapter - BASIC EQUIPMENT|Equipment]] gets [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]] those cards goes into this pile.<br><br>_No worries, if a card is in the Discard Pile, it means that there is a way to gain it back._<br>Instead, if a Cards is lost forever, **[[SoC • Rules - Returning a Card to Your GM|Give it to Your GM]]**.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Rarity***: common, black, red
- ***Color***: artifact
