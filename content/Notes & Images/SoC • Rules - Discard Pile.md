- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>This pile of Cards (_or "Deck", if you are fancy like that_) is for all the cards that where **consumed** or expended, and require [[SoC • Rules - A Night of Rest|A Night of Rest]], [[Heal and Repair|Repair]], or similar to be regained.<br>For example when you lose [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health]], [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]], or a piece of [[SoC • Rules Chapter - BASIC EQUIPMENT|Equipment]] gets [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]] those cards goes into this pile.<br><br>No worries, if a card is in the discard pile, it means that if you give it enough time and rest, it will come back, eventually<br>Instead, if a Cards is lost forever, **[[SoC • Rules - Returning a Card to Your GM|Give it to Your GM]]**.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Rarity***: common, red
- ***Color***: artifact
