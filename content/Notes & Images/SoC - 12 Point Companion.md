A tiger, wolf, bear, or condor is considered the largest of companions. Also in this group are trainee human fighters, equipment squires, chroniclers or personal chefs. The ***GM*** should be prepared to manage the mechanics and story for such a companion, without allowing them to consume more table time than any other character type.<br>The *defaults* for a ***12 Point Companion*** are:
- **4** [[Companions#Companion Skill List|Companion Skills]] at **+3**.
- If it is trained to [[SoC • Companion's Skill - Attack|Attack]], and it hits, it deals **1d8** damage.
- **12** [[Armor Class (AC)|AC]].
- [[Enemy Primer|HP]]: **15**.
