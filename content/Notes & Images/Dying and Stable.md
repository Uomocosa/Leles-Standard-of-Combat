### Dying
If you have no more [[Health|Health Cards]] left, you start **dying**. <br>A **Dying** Creature, falls to the ground [[Unconscious]].
At the end of each round (_including the round you fall unconscious_) you need to ***roll a [[#Death Saving Throw]]*** (_unless you somehow become [[#Stable]]_), and you are [[Unconscious]].

A [[Medicine]] [[Skill Check|check]] of **15** can stabilize a [[Dying and Stable|Dying]] creature.

If you are [[Heal and Repair|Healed]], you stop [[Dying and Stable|Dying]] and also come back to your senses. Once you do so, if you had [[Prepare to Act|Prepared No Actions]] at the start of the [[Phases of Combat|Round]] (_since you were [[Unconscious]]_) you can prepare one now.

Also while you are [[Dying and Stable|Dying]], or [[Dying and Stable|Stable]]:<br>You are [[Unconscious]], so all attacks that are not [[The Extremes|critical failures]] will reach you (_you fail all [[Opposing Rolls]] to [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|Defend]]_). Since you have no health left to loose, for all attacks that hit you, you will add **1** [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]] (at 3 or more you [[Death|Die]]).<br>If you were [[Dying and Stable|Stable]] and gain a [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]], you start [[Dying and Stable|Dying]] yet again.

### Stable
You have no remaining [[Health|Health Cards]], and are still [[Unconscious]]. However you are no longer [[#Dying]], you don't need to roll [[Dying and Stable|Death Saving Throws]] at the end of every [[Phases of Combat|Round]].

### Death Saving Throw
While you are [[#Dying]], at the end of every [[Phases of Combat|Round]] roll a **d20**:
- **1** | Gain **2** [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Cards]], the moment your character reaches **3 or more** *failed death saving throws marks*, he/she is **[[Death|Dead]]**.
- **2-9** | Gain **1** [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]].
- **10-19** | _Nothing happens_.
- **20** | You miraculously come back to your senses. Also gain **1** permanent [[Health|Health Card]].
