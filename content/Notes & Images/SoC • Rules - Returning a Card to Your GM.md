- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>If you loose a piece of [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM GEAR|Gear]], you mysteriously forget a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spell]], or more generally by any means you can no longer use a certain card, you give that card to your GM.<br><br>Loosing a **card** means loosing your progress, it feels terrible.<br>As a **GM**, you should compensate in some ways, in a **fair way**. For example, if your Players menage to recuperate all broken pieces of a [[SoC • Rules - Destroyed Gear|Destroyed Gear]], give back to the owner enough points to build it back. (_He/She can still choose to spend this points in other ways_).
- ***Flavor***: Most importantly as a **GM**: ***DON'T BE A DICK!***. Especially when talking about [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]. 
- ***Rarity***: uncommon 
- ***Color***: artifact, black
