# Example — Creating a Brand New Spell
Let's look at our [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Runes]], and decide which one we want to use.
Let's start with a **Rune of [[SoC • Rune - Telepathy|Telepathy]]**:
![[SoC • Rune - Telepathy|Telepathy]]

**Let's make it simple and use one of the examples provided!** — "Succeed in an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] _[[SoC • Rule - Anima|Anima]] vs. [[Will]]_ against a target Creature in your [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]], to read its thoughts for **1 hour**. Every [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Round]] repeat the same [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]], if you Fail the Spell ends."

Since we used only **1 Rune** we must pay **1 Energy** to cast the following Spell:
![[SoC • Spell - Sanctified Insight|Sanctified Insight]]

Now you can further modify it, for example you can make it an [[SoC • Rune - Area|Area]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spell]] if you have the corresponding Rune, so that it target all Creatures in a Zone.<br>Or you can increase the potency of the [[SoC • Rune - Telepathy|Telepathy]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Effect]] to gain [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on the roll.
**The possibility are endless, but before anything be sure your GM approves of your final spell!**
