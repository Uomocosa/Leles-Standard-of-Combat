The moment you have no [[Health]] left, is the moment you start **dying**.
If you are not **healed** (via a health potion, some spells) when the next turn starts, you need to ***[[#^death-saving-throw|Roll a death saving throw]]*** (unless somehow you become [[#^stable-condition|Stable]]), and you are [[Unconsious]].

Also while you are [[Dying|Stable]] or [[Dying]]:
- You are [[Unconsious]], so all attacks that are not [[The Extremes|critical failures]] will reach you, no metter your [[Armor Class (AC)]], however you have no health left to loose, for all attacks that hurt you will add **1** *failed death saving throw mark* (at 3 or more you [[Death|Die]]).

----
- ***Death Saving Throw***: you are dying, you are fighting for your life.<br>Throw a [[Making Rolls|flat]] `d20` (no bonuses), then depending on the result you'll be closer or further from death. ^death-saving-throw
	- **1** | Add **2** *failed death saving throw marks*, the moment your character reaches **3 or more** *failed death saving throws marks*, he/she is **[[Death|Dead]]**.
	- **2-9** | Add **1** *failed death saving throw mark*.
	- **10-19** | Add or mark **1** *succesful death saving throw mark*, if your character reaches **3 or more** *succesful death saving throw marks*, he/she is [[#^stable-condition|Stable]], meaning you no longer need to throw this roll at the start of each turn, but you are still [[Unconsious]].
	- **20** | You miraculasy come back to your senses, cancel all *failed death saving throw mark*, and gain **1** permanent [[Health]].<br>If you're playing with a **Card System** add the following card to your [[State Deck]]:
		- **1 × [[SoC • Health - Character's Health (1)|Character's Health (1)]]**.
----
- ***Stable Condition***: you are no longer dying, but you are still [[Unconsious]].  ^stable-condition
