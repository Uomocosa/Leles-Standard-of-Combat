- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>You can choose to create a new equipment if you have the necessary components (_enough [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]_).<br>Choose one piece of [[SoC • Rules Chapter - BASIC EQUIPMENT|Equipment]] by spending **1 hour** to work on it, you then make a [[Forging]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] with a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] of **10 + 3 times the cost of the equipment**, if you succeed you can spend **Hero Points** to pay its cost and obtain the **equipment**. Failing the [[Forging]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] won't consume any [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]].<br>Other members of your party could ask you to craft a piece of Equipment for them, to pay its cost you can ask them to give you the necessary **Hero Points**.<br>The [[SoC • Rules Chapter - BASIC EQUIPMENT|Basic Equipment]] and [[Basic Item List|Basic Items]] lists, are to be taken as a reference, and **if your GM allows**, you can come up with completely new ones.
