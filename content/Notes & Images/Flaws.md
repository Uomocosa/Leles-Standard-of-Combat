Things that make you unique, but also prove challenging in time... flaws! 
Each flaw earns **5 points** (unless otherwise stated), **maximum 4 flaws** at character creation.

----
### Character Flaws List
1. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Addict|Addict]]*** — _You have a specific vice you cannot deny_.
2. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Ascetic|Ascetic]]*** — _You frown on possessions_.
3. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Ancient|Ancient]]*** — _You are old!_.
4. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Bad Reputation|Bad reputation]]*** — _You’ve done things... terrible things, and people know about it_.
5. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Crazy|Crazy]]*** — _When faced with a difficult choice, choose randomly_.
6. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Disorganized|Disorganized]]*** — _To you most things are difficult to find_.
7. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Drunkard|Drunkard]]*** — _You cannot renounce alcohol_.
8. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Employed|Employed]]*** — _You are paid or oathsworn to a lord or employer_.
9. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Greedy|Greedy]]*** — _Alwasy tempted by treasure_.
10. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Grudge|Grudge]]*** — _You have been wronged, and hold it against an individual or group, unjustly_.
11. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Impetuos|Impetuous]]*** — _You are impatient, leaping into situations without planning or reservation_.
12. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Injured|Injured]]*** — _A grevious wound to the hand, a scar on your eye_.
13. **[[SoC • Flaw - Just a Kid|Just a kid]]** — _Hey, I’m just a kid!_.
14. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Paranoid|Paranoid]]*** — _You invent your own boogie men, and sew doubt among others_.
15. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Phobia|Phobia]]*** — _Extream fear of a common thing_.
16. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Pursued|Pursued]]*** — _You have a nemesis out there... somewhere... hunting you_.
17. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Sickly|Sickly]]*** — _You were born frail, but that didn't stop you from adventuring_.
18. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Stubborn|Stubborn]]*** — _Once you make up your mind it is really difficult to convince you otherwise_.
19. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Timid|Timid]]*** — _You’d rather let others lead_.
20. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Savior|Savior]]*** — _Your spells are for others, never for you_.
21. ***[[SoC • Flaw - Unluky|Unlucky]]*** — _You fail spectacularly more often than others_.
