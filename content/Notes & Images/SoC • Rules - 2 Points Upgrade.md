- ***Description***:<br>**2 Points Upgrade** — You can **Upgrade your Gear** by paying **2 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**, and inscribe a **two [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Runes]]**, with an Effect that has duration of **1 hour** or more, and use it as an **Upgrade**. Otherwise, the first time this weapon would become [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]], instead it does not.<br>• _[[SoC • Rune - Sensory|Sensory]], [[SoC • Rune - Sensory|Sensory]]_ — While having this **Gear** **Equipped** you see see all **Invisible** objects and Creatures **in your Zone**.
