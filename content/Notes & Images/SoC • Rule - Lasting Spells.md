- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>Whenever you cast a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spell]] that costs **1 pt or less**, you may choose to spend **1 additional [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Point]]**, along with the usual [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy]] cost.<br>If you do, any effect of the spell that would normally last **1 hour** instead becomes **permanent**.
- **_Flavor_**: Note — A permanent spell can still be [[SoC • Rune - Dispel|Dispelled]], or end due to other effects or conditions, just not due to the passage of time.
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Color***: artifact
