*You fall to the ground, your eyes close, and all your senses become more and more distant, like echoes fading into silence. The world around you dissolves into a formless void as your body succumbs to the pull of darkness, leaving you **vulnerable** and **unaware***.

While you are **Unconsious**:
- You are [[Unconsious]], so all attacks that are not [[The Extremes|critical failures]] will reach you, no metter your [[Armor Class (AC)]].
- **At the start of each turn** you can [[set down a card|set]] one card less than you normally could (usually this means that you can only set 1 card down for the turn, instead of 2).<br>**And** you can use the set card/cards only if you become conscious again during the turn.
- If you are not [[Dying]] or [[Dying|Stable]], **at the start of each turn** and **every time you receive damage** you can try to wake up by rolling a [[Skills|Skill Check (Will)]], against a **DC (Difficutly Class)** decided by your GM.
