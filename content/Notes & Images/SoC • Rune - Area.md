- ***Info***: Rune - Area 
- ***Description***:<br>You can modify a single-target Spell to affect an entire **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]]** or more. However, the expanded area is not completely saturated by the Spell's Effect, meaning the targets within it have can try to avoid being affected. **You can choose to be included or excluded by the Spell Effect**.<br>Succeed in an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]]: _[[SoC • Rule - Anima|Anima]] **vs.** [[Agility]]_ to apply the Spell's Effects.<br>**Num. of Area Runes** | Example of Effect:<br>• **1** | The spell now affects all Creatures in your same **Zone**.<br>• **2** | The spell now affects all Creatures in your same **Zone**, roll your [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] with **[[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]]**.<br>• **3** | The spell now affects all Creatures in a radius of **2 Zones**.<br>• **4** | The spell now affects all Creatures in a radius of **2 Zones**, roll your [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] with **[[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]]**.<br>• **4** | The spell now affects all Creatures in a radius of **3 Zones**.
