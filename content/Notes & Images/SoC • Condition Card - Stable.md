- ***Info***: Condition
- ***Description***:<br>You have no remaining [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health Cards]], and are still [[Unconscious]]. However you are no longer [[#Dying]], you don't need to roll [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Death Saving Throws]] at the end of every [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Round]].<br>Also while you are [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Stable]]: you are [[Unconscious]], so all attacks that are not [[SoC • Rules - Critical Success and Failure|Critical Failures]] will reach you (_you fail all [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Rolls]] to [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|Defend]]_). Since you have no health left to loose, for all attacks that hit you, you will add **1** [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]] (at 3 or more you [[SoC • Rules - Death|Die]]).<br>If you gain a [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]], you start [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Dying]] yet again.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Color***: artifact, white
- ***Rarity***: common
