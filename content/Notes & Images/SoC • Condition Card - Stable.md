- ***Info***: Condition
- ***Description***:<br>You have no remaining [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health Cards]], and are still **[[Unconscious]]**. However **you are no longer [[#Dying]]**, you don't need to Roll [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Death Saving Throws]] at the end of every [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Round]].<br><br>**While you are [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Stable]]** — You are **[[Unconscious]]**, so you'll automatically Fail all [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Checks]] except [[Will]] [[SoC • Rules - Skill Checks|Skill Checks]]. Since you have no [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health]] left to lose, for any Attack that reaches you: gain **1 [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]]** (_at 3 or more you **[[SoC • Rules - Death|Die]]**_).<br>_Barely Stable_ — If you gain a **[[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]]** (_like from an Attack_) you stop being **Stable** and begin **Dying** again.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Color***: artifact, white
- ***Rarity***: common
