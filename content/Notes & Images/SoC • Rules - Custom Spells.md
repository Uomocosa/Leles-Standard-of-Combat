- ***Nameplate***: Custom Spells
- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>_When [[Basic Spells]] will no longer fulfill the desired level of magical craft, you are ready to create spells from scratch, or modify a spell you have already learned. Before customizing a Spell try to have a clear idea of what you want to achive._<br>To Customize a Spell:<br>• Spend **1 or more points** to add a new effect to the spell, or increase the potency of a previously added effect.<br>• If you wish, you can add a **Limitation**, reducing the Spell cost by **1**. A spell cannot cost less then **1 pts**.<br>• **You cannot spend more than 5 pts on a single Spell (calculated before applying any Limitations)**. _Spells more powerful exists but can only be created by extremely powerful magicians_.<br>• To create or modify a Spell you need to experiment on it for **1 hour**. After which you need to succeed in a [[Arcane]], [[Faith]] or [[Nature]] [[Skill Checks]] against a [[Skill Checks|DC]] of **10 + 3 times the cost of the spell (calculated before applying any Limitations)**.
