- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>When a new **Campaign** starts all the Characters get some **Hero Points** (usually **20**) to create and customize their characters.<br>Each Character and Companion gets **5 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health Cards]]** and **3 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ENERGY|Energy Cards]]**, for free, as well as some **Generic Action Cards**, a **[[SoC • Card - Character Details|Character Details]] Card**, and **Rations**.<br><br>How many **Points**, **Health** and **Energy**, ..., each Character gets at the start, is ultimately decided (as most things are) by your **GM**.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Color***: artifact, multicolor
