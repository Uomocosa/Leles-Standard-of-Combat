All [[Custom Equipment|Gear]] has an health value.<br>For example: ***Helmet Health: 2, 2, 5***.<br>This value represents the corresponding [[Health|Health Cards]] you can add to your [[Deck]], for this particular helmet, you will add the following cards to your [[Deck]]:
- **2 × [[SoC • Health - Helmet Health (2)]]**.
- **1 × [[SoC • Health - Helmet Health (5)]]**.

Your [[Custom Equipment|Gear]] can help you prevent damage, when [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|taking damage]] you can [[expend]] your **Equiped Gears's Health** as you would for your [[Health|Character's Health]].

*(Optional Rule) — Frail Equipment*:<br>When a certain piece of [[Custom Equipment|Gear]] reaches 0 health, it is [[Destroyed Gear|Destroyed]]. ^optional-rule-frail-equipment
