*You try to defend against a dragon with: A wooden shield, hopes and prayers.
Your GM has decided that what you have left are just prayers, your shield is **destroyed**, what it remains are just ashes, [[returning a card to your GM|give the shield card to your GM]]*.

When your GM decides that a certain piece of [[Custom Equipment|Gear]] has reached such a breaking point that no amount of [[Custom Spells|Spells]], nor [[Forging]] can [[Heal and Repair|Repair]] it, that piece of gear is **Destroyed**, [[returning a card to your GM|give the Equipment card to your GM]].

>As a **GM** you _should_ give back some or all [[Hero Points]] back the Equipment previous owner, over the course of some sessions.

> *(Optional Rule) — Leave it to Fate*:<br>Every time an equipment gets [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]], make the owner roll a **d20**, if the result is a [[The Extremes|critical failure]], the Equipment is **Destroyed**. ^optional-leave-it-to-fate
