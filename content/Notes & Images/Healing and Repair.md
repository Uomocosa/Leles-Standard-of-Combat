> ***When you regain [[Health]] (from heals or repairs) you move a certain number of [[Type of Cards|Health Cards]] from your [[Discard Pile]] to your [[State Deck]]***.

Upon a successful ***heal*** (by magic, or potion), you instantly **ragain** **1** or more [[Health|Character's Health Cards]], specified by the spell effect.
- If you some have time to spare, you can try to **heal** using the [[Medicine]] skill.
- After a [[Rest & Recovery|a night of rest]] you regain all [[Health|Character's Health]].

For [[Damaged Gear|Damaged Gear and Equipment]], you can use a spell to instantly regain **1** or more [[Equiped Gears's Health|Gear's Health Cards]], specified by the spell effect.
- If you have time to spare, you can try to use the [[Forging]] skill. 
- ***While, [[Destroyed Gear]] cannot be repaired, and will need to replaced entirely***.
