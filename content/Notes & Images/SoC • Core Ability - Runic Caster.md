You cannot add [[SoC • Discord Bot - Thread • CUSTOM SPELLS|Limitation]] to these spells.<br>• ***Mystic Lexicon*** — Whenever you could [[Meditate and Train|Meditate]], you can instead pay **3 pts** and choose a new [[SoC • Discord Bot - Thread • CUSTOM SPELLS|Effect]] to inscribe it as a rune. You cannot inscribe Runes for the [[SoC • Spell Effect - Lasting|Lasting]] or [[SoC • Spell Effect - Passive|Passive]] [[SoC • Discord Bot - Thread • CUSTOM SPELLS|Effect]].<br>• ***Sigil Forging*** — You can also create [[SoC • Introduction - Custom Spells|Custom Spells]] with your Runes and imbue them in [[Scrolls and Potions]].
