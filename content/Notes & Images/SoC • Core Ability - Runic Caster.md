- ***Description***:<br>_You’ve discovered a unique way to channel magical power, crafting spells tailored to each situation. You are able to inscribe on your body, in a book, or on any surface you carry rune carrying magical power_. Each rune represents to a [[Custom Spells|Spell Effect]].<br>When you take this [[Core Ability]] choose **3** [[Custom Spells|Spell Effects]] (except [[SoC • Spell Effect - Lasting|Lasting]] or [[SoC • Spell Effect - Passive|Passive]]) to inscribe them as runes.<br>You also gain the following effects:<br>• ***Runic Casting*** — At any time, you can create a [[Custom Spells|Custom Spell]] using the [[Custom Spells|Effects]] of your runes and cast it, this spell follows the same rules as any other Custom Spell, you still need to pay its **Energy Cost**. You cannot add [[Custom Spells|Limitation]] to these spells.<br>• ***Mystic Lexicon*** — Whenever you could [[Meditate and Train|study]], you can instead pay **3 pts** and choose a new [[Custom Spells|Effect]] to inscribe it as a rune. You cannot inscribe runes for the [[SoC • Spell Effect - Lasting|Lasting]] or [[SoC • Spell Effect - Passive|Passive]] [[Custom Spells|Effect]].<br>• ***Sigil Forging*** — You can also create custom spells with your runes and imbue them in [[Scrolls and Potions]].
- ***Flavor***: This ability is recommended for experienced players, as creating custom spells can slow gameplay. Consult your **GM** before selecting this core ability.
