>***Skill Check***: `d20` + [[Skill Checks|Skill Modifier]], againt a certain [[Skill Checks|DC]] decided by your **GM**, it is successul if the total result is equal or greater than the [[Skill Checks|DC]].

Also whenever you roll one or more `d20`s and add a [[Skill Checks|Skill]] modifier to a roll, that roll is also considered a **Skill Check**.
*For example, an [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|Attack Roll]] is also a **Skill Check**, however a [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|Damage Roll]] is NOT a **Skill Check**, since it does not roll a `d20`*.
