- ***Info***: Rune - Sensory 
- ***Description***:<br>A magic effect that act on one, ore more senses of a Creature, in your same **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]]**, for **1 hour**.<br>**Num. of Sensory Runes** | Example of Effect:<br>• **1** | For **1 hour** see through **Darkness**, as you would during the day.<br>• **1** | For **1 hour** see what another Creature sees, or vice versa, an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] might be needed.<br>• **2** | For **1 hour** see all **Invisible** objects and Creatures **in your [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]]**.<br>• **2** | Choose a **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill]]**, and a Creature **in your [[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]]**, for **1 hour** the next time that creature would roll a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Check]] with that Skill, it does so with [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]].<br>Similar magical effects don't comulate, you can gain **multiple [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantages]]** on the same [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill]], however each [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] has to be given by different means (_like a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CORE ABILITIES|Core Abilities]]_).
