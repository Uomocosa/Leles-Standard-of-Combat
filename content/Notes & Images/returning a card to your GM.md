If you loose a piece of [[Custom Equipment|Gear]], you mysteriously forget a [[SoC • Discord Bot - Thread • CUSTOM SPELLS|Spell]], or more generally by any means you can no longer use a certain card, you give that card to your GM.

Loosing a **card** means loosing your progress, it feels terrible.
As a **GM**, you should compensate in some ways, in a **fair way**. For example, if your Players menage to recuperate all broken pieces of a [[Destroyed Gear]], give back to the owner enough points to build it back. (_He/She can still choose to spend this points in other ways_).
Most importantly as a **GM**: ***DON'T BE A DICK!***. Especially when talking about [[SoC • Discord Bot - Thread • NEW CAMPAIGN]].
