If a Scared Creature begins its Turn in the same Zone as the source of its fears it must [[SoC • Generic Action - Movement or Skill|Move]] at least one Zone away from it.
A Scared Creature cannot voluntarily [[SoC • Generic Action - Movement or Skill|Move]] in the same Zone as the source of its fears.

If you are Scared you can [[Forgo an Action]] to try and calm yourself. 
- If the source of fear is a creature you can [[Forgo an Action]] to make an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|opposing roll]]: _[[Will]] **vs.** [[Intimidation]]_ to calm yourself.
- Otherwise Succeed in a [[Will]] [[Skill Checks]] against a [[Skill Checks|DC]] decided by your **GM** to end the fear.
