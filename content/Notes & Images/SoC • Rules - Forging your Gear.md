- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>If you have the necessary components (_**[[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**_), you can spend **1 hour** to work on a piece of **Gear** you wish to **Crate** or **Upgrade**.<br><br>You then make a [[Forging]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] with a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] of **10 + 3 times the total cost of the equipment**, if you succeed you can spend the **points** to obtain the **equipment**. (_Failing the [[Forging]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] won't consume any [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]_.)<br><br>If you wish to **[[Heal and Repair|Repair]]** a piece of equipment the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] is lower: **5 + 3 times the total cost of the equipment**, and it wont require any **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**.<br><br>If your **GM** allows it, you can also come up with entirely new pieces of Equipment!
- ***Flavor***: Example — If you want to upgrade your [[SoC • Basic Weapon - Dagger|Dagger]] (that costs **1 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Point]]**) gear with a **[[SoC • Rules - 2 Points Upgrade|2 Points Upgrade]]** its total cost would be **3**, so the [[Forging]] **DC** would be **19**.
- ***Color***: artifact, red 
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Image Filter***: spell_effect
