- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>If you have the required components (_**[[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**_), you can spend **1 hour** to work on a piece of **Gear** you wish to **Create** or **Upgrade**.<br><br>Make a **[[Forging]]** [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] with a **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]] equal to 10 + 3× the total Cost of the Equipment**.<br>If you succeed, you may spend the corresponding **Hero Points** to complete the Gear. (_Failing the check does not consume any Hero Points._)<br><br>To **[[Heal and Repair|Repair]]** [[Damaged Gear]], the **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|DC]]** is the same (_**10 + 3× the total Cost of the Equipment**_), but **no Hero Points are required**.<br><br>If your **GM** allows it, you can invent entirely new Equipment as well.

- ***Color***: artifact, red 
- ***Rarity***: common
