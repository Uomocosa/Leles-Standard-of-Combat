  - total effect cost
---
Given a [[Custom Equipment|custom gear]] or [[Custom Spells|custom spell]], its total effect-cost is equal to the [[Hero Points|hero points]] spent on the **effects ALONE**, so before applying any **limitation**.
If a **spell** has a [[Basic Spells|Basic Spell]] as it bases, that basic spell cost is also added to the calculation, like if was an additional **effect**.

----
*~Example:* The [[SoC • Spell - Erratic Ally|Erratic Ally]] spell, created with the effects: [[SoC • Rune - Duration|Duration]], [[SoC • Rune - Summon|Summon-2]], and limitations: [[SoC • Spell Limitiation - Maddening|Maddening]], [[SoC • Spell Limitiation - Ritual|Ritual]], [[SoC • Spell Limitiation - Unstable|Unstable]].<br>Has a ***total effect cost*** of **9 pts**, to brake it down in more details:
- [[SoC • Rune - Duration|Duration]]: **3 pts**.
- [[SoC • Rune - Summon|Summon-2]]: **6 pts**.
----
*~Example:* The [[SoC • Spell - Erratic Wall|Erratic Wall]] spell, created with the effects: [[SoC • Basic Spell - Invisible Wall|Invisible Wall]], [[SoC • Rune - Animate|Animate]], [[SoC • Rune - Duration|Duration]], and limitations: [[SoC • Spell Limitiation - Maddening|Maddening]], [[SoC • Spell Limitiation - Ritual|Ritual]], [[SoC • Spell Limitiation - Unstable|Unstable]].<br>Has a ***total effect cost*** of **9 pts**, to brake it down in more details:
- [[SoC • Basic Spell - Invisible Wall|Invisible Wall]]: **3 pts**.
- [[SoC • Rune - Animate|Animate]]: **3 pts**.
- [[SoC • Rune - Duration|Duration]]: **3 pts**.
----
