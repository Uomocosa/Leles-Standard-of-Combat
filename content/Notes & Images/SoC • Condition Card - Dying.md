- ***Description***:<br>If you have no more [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health Cards]] left, you start **dying**. <br>A **Dying** Creature, falls to the ground [[Unconscious]].<br>At the end of each round (_including the round you fall unconscious_) you need to ***roll a [[#Death Saving Throw]]*** (_unless you somehow become [[#Stable]]_), and you are [[Unconscious]].<br>A [[Medicine]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|check]] of **15** can [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Stabilize]] a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Dying]] creature.<br>If you are [[Heal and Repair|Healed]], you stop [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Dying]] and also come back to your senses. Once you do so, if you had [[SoC • Rules - Prepare to Act|Prepared No Actions]] at the start of the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Round]] (_since you were [[Unconscious]]_) you can prepare one now.<br>Also while you are [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Dying]]: you are [[Unconscious]], so all attacks that are not [[SoC • Rules - Critical Success and Failure|Critical Failures]] will reach you (_you fail all [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Rolls]] to [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|Defend]]_). Since you have no health left to loose, for all attacks that hit you, you will add **1** [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]] (at 3 or more you [[SoC • Rules - Death|Die]]).
