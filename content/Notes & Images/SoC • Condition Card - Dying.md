- ***Description***:<br>If you have no more [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health Cards]] left, you start **Dying**. You Immediately falls to the ground and becomes **[[Unconscious]]**.<br>**At the end of each [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Round]]** — A Dying Creature must _**Roll a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Death Saving Throw]]**_.<br>**While you are [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Dying]]** — You are **[[Unconscious]]**, so you'll automatically Fail all [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Checks]] except [[Will]] [[SoC • Rules - Skill Checks|Skill Checks]]. Since you have no [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Health]] left to lose, for any Attack that reaches you: gain **1 [[SoC • Generic Card - Closer to Death|Closer to Death Card]]** (_at 3 or more you **[[SoC • Rules - Death|Die]]**_).<br><br>A **[[Medicine]]** [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Check]] of **15** can **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Stabilize]]** a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Dying]] Creature.<br>If you are **[[Heal and Repair|Healed]]**, you stop [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|Dying]] and also come back to your senses. Once you do so, if you had [[SoC • Rules - Prepare to Act|Prepared No Actions]] at the start of the [[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Round]] (_since you were [[Unconscious]]_) you can prepare one now.
