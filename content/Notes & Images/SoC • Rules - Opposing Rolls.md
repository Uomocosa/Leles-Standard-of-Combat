- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>An Opposing Roll is made between **two or more Creatures**.<br>Each Creature involved is either **the attacker** or **a defender**. If a defender rolls **higher** than the attacker then, _usually_, nothing happens to the defender. (_If the two rolls are **equal**, than the **attacker** wins the roll_)<br><br>_Here are some examples_:<br>• ***Melee Attack*** — Succeed in an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]]: _[[Might]] **vs.** [[Might]] or [[Agility]]_ (_defender uses lowest_) to hit.<br>• ***Ranged Attack*** — Succeed in an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]]: _[[Agility]] **vs.** [[Agility]]_ to hit. For each **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - ZONES|Zone]]** between you and your target, roll with **an additional [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]]**.<br>• ***Spell Effect*** — If the target tries to resist, Succeed in an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]]: _[[SoC • Rule - Anima|Anima]] **vs.** [[Will]]_ to apply the Spell's Effects. At the end of **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - COMBAT|Round]]** Succeed in the same Opposing Roll, otherwise the Spell ends.
- ***Flavor***: 
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Color***: artifact, white
