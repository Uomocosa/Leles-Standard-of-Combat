1. ***Spellblast***: *Explosion! Absorb **2** [[Combat Damage|Damages]]. The blast is large, reaching to all creature adjecent to you*.
2. ***Wild Shot***: *The spell fires at a random target or area, including allies*.
3. ***Forgetful***: *You forget how to cast this spell for **1d4** days. For each of those days, roll [[Investigation]] to try and remember*.
4. ***Psychic Spike***: *Your mind reels from failed magic. Take a **-3 penalty** to your [[Arcane]] [[Skills|Skill]] until your next [[Rest & Recovery|rest]]*.
5. ***Erosion***: *The spell itself is damaged. Randomly add a new [[Custom Spells#Spell Limitation List|limitation]]*.
6. ***Echo***: *A searing, loud noise erupts, heard for miles*.
7. ***A dud***: *The cast fails, and expends itself*.
8. ***Stable***: *Your spell casts normally, with no unstable aspects*.
