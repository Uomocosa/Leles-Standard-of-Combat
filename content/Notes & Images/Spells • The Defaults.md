If you’re playing a magic-user, you have a wealth of possibilities ahead. You can choose from [[Basic Spells|basic spells]], let your ***GM*** offer available spells to buy with points as you progress, or [[Custom Spells|create your custom spells]] for the unmatched fun of bespoke magical effects. 
Whether you use premade spells, build them custom, or discover them as equipment in the form of wands, staves, rings or scrolls, all spells follow a few core rules, almost the same as '_CROWN and SKULL_'.

----
### Critical Concepts for All Spells
- ***Casts and Expend***: Spells are cast on command. By default, spells include **1 cast**. Once a spell has no casts remaining, it is expended and must be studied again in rest and safety to cast again. Add [[SoC • Rune - Recast|Additional casts]] with *[[Custom Spells|customization]]*.
- ***Magic Skills***: [[Arcane]] is rolled to contain [[SoC • Spell Limitation - Unstable|Unstable spells]], while [[Faith]] if a spell [[SoC • Spell Limitation - Uses Infernal Powers|Uses infernal powers]], they are is also used as a defensive measure against enemy spells and effects in some cases. Or to see if your spell succesfully hits a target, and sometimes to deal more damage.
- ***Somatics***: By default, **all spells require audible words and gestures with BOTH HANDS to cast**. This must be remembered when casting under duress, when using equipment, or when speech is suppressed or disrupted.
- ***Duration***: The default duration of a spell’s effect is **1** [[Phases of Combat|round]]. If an effect is to conclude on the caster’s action phase, it dissipates at the end of that phase. Upgrade effect [[SoC • Spell Effect - Duration|Duration]] with *[[Custom Spells|customization]]*.
- ***Effect***: The default effect output of a spell is **1d6** . This could be damage, **for example**. This amount can be [[SoC • Rune - Upgrade|Upgraded]] with *[[Custom Spells|customization]]*.
- ***Range***: All spells require **touch** as the default range to cast. This can be upgraded to [[SoC • Spell Effect - Ranged|Ranged]] or [[SoC • Spell Effect - Area|Area]] with *[[Custom Spells|customization]]*.
- ***Targets***: In their default state, all spells affect a **single target**. A spell can be upgraded to affect **multiple targets** or **areas** with *[[Custom Spells|customization]]*.
- ***Inscribing spells***: All spells that are not [[SoC • Spell Limitation - Ineffable|Ineffable]], can be recorded and **inscribed** for later use.<br>The inscription of a spell [[Hero Points|costs]] **1 pt** for each [[Custom Spells|Effect]] that spell has (minimum **1 pt**).
