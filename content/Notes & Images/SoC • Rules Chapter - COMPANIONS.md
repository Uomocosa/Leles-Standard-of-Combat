# Example — Getting a Companion
_I want a horse!_ So, let us get one.
We start as a "blank" Companion, by spending **2 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**. We ask our **GM** if we can get a Horse, it agrees without any problem, and it also tells us that a Horse can also transport up to **2 Medium-sized Creatures**, nice!<br>Finally, like we did for our Character, let's choose a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Rune]] for our Companion, we'll go with a **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Rune]] of [[SoC • Rune - Haste|Haste]]**.
![[SoC • Companion - Horse|Horse]]

But let's make it more ours and choose to give it a nice [[SoC • Rule - Passive Ability|Passive Ability]]. Using **2 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Runes]] of [[SoC • Rune - Haste|Haste]]**, we can make it much more fast! Let's also give it a name, so it will be more devastating when our **GM** kills it.
