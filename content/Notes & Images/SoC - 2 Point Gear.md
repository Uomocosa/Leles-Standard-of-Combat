Medium-sized weapons, light armor, flares, bear traps & snares, thieves’ tools, common supplies.

The *defaults* for ***2 Point Weapon or Armor*** are:
- [[Basic Armaments List#Weapons|Weapon]]: damage: **1d6**, [[Health]]: **2**, **1**.
- [[Basic Armaments List#Weapons|Weapon]]: damage: **1d6**, [[Health]]: **2**.<br>You can use [[Agility]] instead of [[Force]] for [[Combat Damage|Attack Roll]] made with this weapons.
- [[Basic Armaments List#Weapons|Weapon]]: damage: **1d4**, [[Health]]: **1**, **1**, **1**.<br>You can use [[Agility]] instead of [[Force]] for [[Combat Damage|Attack Roll]] made with this weapons.
- [[Basic Armaments List#Armors|Armors and Shields]]: bonus CA: **+1**, [[Health]]: **3**, **2**.
