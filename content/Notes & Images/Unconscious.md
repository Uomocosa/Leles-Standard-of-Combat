_You fall to the ground, your eyes close, and all your senses become more and more distant, like echoes fading into silence. The world around you dissolves into a formless void as your body succumbs to the pull of darkness, leaving you **vulnerable** and **unaware**_.

While you are **Unconscious**:
- **You cannot [[Prepare to Act|prepare any action]]**.
- **You fail all [[Skill Check|Skill Checks]]**, except for [[Will]] [[Skill Check|Skill Checks]].
- Depending on the situation, your **GM** decide how difficult it is for you to regain you senses, sometimes you just need an ally to shake you, other times only spells can wake you up.

If you are brought back to your senses, (_so the [[Unconscious]] condition ends_), and had [[Prepare to Act|Prepared No Actions]] at the start of the [[Phases of Combat|Round]], you can prepare one now.
