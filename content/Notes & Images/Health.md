> Your ***Health*** is defined by how many [[Type of Cards|Health Cards]] are in your [[State Deck]].<br>There are **3** "normal" values for Health Cards: **[[SoC • Health - Character's Health (1)|(1)]]**, **[[SoC • Health - Character's Health (2)|(2)]]** and **[[SoC • Health - Character's Health (3)|(3)]]**.
> When you take damage, you need to [[Discard Pile|discard]] a certain number of [[Type of Cards|Health Cards]] with a total value **equal or greater** than the damage you took.<br>_Example — Your character still has the following [[Type of Cards|Health Cards]]: **3**, **2**, **1**, **1**.<br>You take **5 damage**, you [[Discard Pile|discard]] one of your **3**s and one **2** cards, you're left with **3**, **1** health_.

Each character starts with an [[Health]] of **1**, **1**, **1**.
Meaning you add **3 copies of [[SoC • Health - Character's Health (1)|Character's Health (1)]]** to your [[State Deck]].
(*This health is for free, due to the basic-creature-rights of having some health*)

There are two types of **Health** or **Health Cards**:<br>They are aquired, and regained in different ways, but have basically the same function: to prevent you from [[Dying and Stable]].<br>**REMBER** to not mix the different types of health. I suggest using the **Card System** at least to keep  track of your health.
1. ***Character's Health***:<br>You can make your character more tough, by bying [[Additional Health]] with your [[Hero Points]]. ^characters-health
2. ***Gear's Health***:<br>Each [[Equipment List|piece of Armor, Shield and Weapon]] has its own [[Health]] (or [[Type of Cards|Health Cards]]. ^gears-health

*(Optional Rule) — Simpler Health*:<br>Do not differentiate between [[SoC • Health - Upper Armor Health (1)|Upper Armor Health]], [[SoC • Health - Weapon Health (1)|Weapon Health]], ..., add it all together as **gear health**. ^optional-rule-simpler-health

*(Optional Rule) — Simplest Health*:<br>Do not differentiate between different kinds of healths, add it all together as one single value of **health**. ^optional-rule-simplest-health

### Temporary Health
Some enchantments, or other **sources**, will give you some **Temporary Health**: 
- When you are [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|hit]] you'll first use your **Temporary Health Cards** instead of your normal **Health Cards**.<br>**Temporary Health Cards**, do NOT go to your [[Discard Pile]], [[returning a card to your GM|return them to your GM]].
- **Temporary Health** cannot be comulated on the same **target**, if two **sources** both give **Temporary Health** (to the same **target**), you choose the greater.<br>*If I already have 3 Temporary Health on my Helmet, and another source would give me 2 Temporary Health to the Helmet, my Helmet would not gain anything*.<br>*However if the new source is **stronger** then the current one, things are slightly different, so let's say I already have 3 Temporary Health on my Helmet, and another source would give me 5, my Helmet would go to 5 Temporary Health*.
