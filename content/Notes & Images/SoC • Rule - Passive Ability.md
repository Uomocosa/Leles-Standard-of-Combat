- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>Whenever you **Create or Learn a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spell]]** that costs **2 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]] or less**, you may choose to spend **3 additional [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]** to turn it into a **Passive Ability**.<br>A **Passive Ability** cannot be manually casted, it is always active **on yourself**.<br>• Any of its effects that would normally expire after **1 hour** (or more) **do not expire**.<br>• After [[Rest and Recovery|a Night of Rest]], any limited-use effects (e.g., “Once in the following hour,” “The next time...,” or “The next X times...”) regain all their uses. If the effect ended for any other reason, it is automatically "cast" again at the end of your rest.<br>• **Passive Ability cannot be [[SoC • Rune - Dispel|Dispelled]]**.

- ***Rarity***: uncommon
- ***Color***: artifact, multicolor
