- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>You can spend **1 hour** to Create a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spell]] using **2 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Runes]]**, and pay **5 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**, to turn it into a **Passive Ability**. (_You don't Cast the Spell, and you don't consume Energy_)<br><br>A **Passive Ability** cannot be manually casted, it is always active **on yourself**.<br>• Any of its effects that would normally expire after **1 hour** (or more) **do not expire**.<br>• **After [[SoC • Rules Chapter - HEALTH|a Night of Rest]]** — any limited-use effects (e.g., “Once in the following hour,...”, or  “The next time...,”) regain all their uses. If the effect ended for any other reason, it is automatically _"Cast"_ again at the end of your Rest.<br>• **Passive Abilities cannot be [[SoC • Rune - Dispel|Dispelled]]**.<br>• You can have as many Passive Abilities as you want, as long as their Effects are not similar. (_If in doubt ask your **GM**._)

- ***Rarity***: uncommon
- ***Color***: artifact, multicolor
