- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>Every [[Custom Spells|Effect]] that has a duration of **1 hour** or more, and costs **2** **pts** or less, can be chosen as an **Upgrade**.<br>**Upgrading** — Like a [[SoC • Spell Effect - Passive|Passive Effect]], The **Effect**/**s** chosen as an **Upgrade** will not end and cannot be [[SoC • Spell Effect - Dispel|Dispelled]]. However the **Effect**/**s** chosen as an **Upgrade** can be active on the **Creature** that holds the **Gear**, or on the **Gear** itself (_your choice_). <br>Remember that if a piece of **Gear** is [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]] it will only deal **1** damage and it looses all **Effects**, until it is [[Heal and Repair|Repaired]]. (_meaning it will loose all Upgrades as well, until repaired_)
