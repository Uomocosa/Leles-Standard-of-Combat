- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>Every [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Rune]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Effect]] that has a duration of **1 hour** or more, can be chosen as an **Upgrade**.<br>**Upgrading** — _Infuse a Single [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Rune]] into a piece of [[SoC • Introduction - Gear|Gear]]_. Like a [[SoC • Rule - Passive Ability|Passive Ability]], The **Effect**/**s** chosen as an **Upgrade** will not end and cannot be [[SoC • Rune - Dispel|Dispelled]]. However the **Effect**/**s** chosen as an **Upgrade** can be active on the **Creature** that holds the **Gear**, or on the **Gear** itself (_your choice_). <br>Remember that if a piece of **Gear** is [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]] it will only deal **1** damage and it looses all **Effects**, until it is [[Heal and Repair|Repaired]]. (_meaning it will loose all Upgrades as well, until repaired_)
- ***Flavor***: See [[SoC • Rules - 1 Point Upgrade|1 Point Upgrade]] and [[SoC • Rules - 2 Points Upgrade|2 Points Upgrade]] for the specifics.
- ***Color***: artifact, red 
- ***Rarity***: common
