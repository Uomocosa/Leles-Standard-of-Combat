- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>You may **infuse up to 2 [[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Runes]]** into a piece of [[SoC • Introduction - Gear|Gear]] to **Upgrade** it..<br>Just like a [[SoC • Rule - Passive Ability|Passive Ability]], any Runes Effects that would normally expire after **1 hour or more** become **permanent**, and **cannot be [[SoC • Rune - Dispel|Dispelled]]**.<br>You choose whether the Rune Effects apply to the **Creature** using the Gear or the **Gear** itself.<br><br>To Upgrade a piece of Gear:<br>• Spend **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]** equal to the number of **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - RUNES|Runes]]** used (_rounded up_).<br>• Increase the **Cost** of the Equipment by the same amount.<br>• Spend **1 hour** working on it.<br>• Succeed on a **[[Forging]]** [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Check]] with **DC equal to 10 + 3× total Equipment Cost**. (_Failing doesn’t consume any Hero Points._)
- ***Flavor***:
- ***Color***: artifact, red 
- ***Rarity***: common
- ***Image Filter***: spell_effect

(_failing does not consume **[[SoC • Rules Chapter - NEW CAMPAIGN|Hero Points]]**_)
