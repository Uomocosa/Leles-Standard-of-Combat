*Keep in you inventory all the necessities you need, like [[rations]] and water in a [[flask]], and whatever else you consider might come useful in the future*.

There is no limit on how many cards you can put in your **Inventory** deck.
This is made for convience, so that players don't need keep track of how much weigth they are carrying, please refrain to transport too many items, or items that are clearly too heavy for your character, if in doubt ask your GM.

*(Optional Rule) — Precious Space*:<br>You can carry only a total of cards in your **Inventory** equal to **5** plus the number of [[Skills|Skill Points]] you have put in [[Force]]<br>(**NOTE: add the number of Force Skill Points, not the [[Skills|Force Modifier]]**). ^optional-rule-precious-space

*(Optional Rule) — Precious Space (Variant)*:<br>You can carry only a total of cards in your **Inventory** equal to **6** plus the number of [[Skills|Skill Points]] you have put in [[Force]]<br>If you carry in your [[Inventory]] an [[Basic Equipment List|Item]], [[Basic Armaments List|Armament]] or [[Custom Equipment|Custom Gear]] that has a [[Gear's Requirment|Requirement]] of [[Force]] that you do not meet, then it counts as you having an extra card in your **Inventory**.<br>(**NOTE: add the number of Force Skill Points, not the [[Skills|Force Modifier]]**). ^optional-rule-precious-space
