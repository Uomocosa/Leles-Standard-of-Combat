Now you can further modify it, for example you can make it an [[SoC • Spell Effect - Area|Area]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Spell]] so that it target all Creatures in a Zone.<br>Or you can increase the potency of the [[SoC • Spell Effect - Telepathy|Telepathy]] [[SoC • Rules Chapter - CUSTOM SPELLS|Effect]] to gain [[SoC • Rules - Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on the roll.
