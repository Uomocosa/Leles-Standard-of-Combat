A ferret, rat, snake, parrot, frog, or hedgehog may not have great strength, but they make good company, stow away with ease and generally go unnoticed by foes.<br>The *defaults* for a ***3 Point Companion*** are:
- **1** [[Companions#Companion Skill List|Companion Skill]] at **+3**.
- If it is trained to [[SoC • Companion's Skill - Attack|Attack]], and it hits, it deals **1** damage.
- **6** [[Armor Class (AC)|AC]].
- [[Enemy Primer|HP]]: **3**.
