Dogs, cats, falcons, owls, or even small pigs count as medium-sized. Also in this group are tiny devices such as mechanical animals, orbs or smaller automatons. The latter should be very rare in any campaign. Medium companions have more combat capability, but also become targets.<br>The defaults for a ***5 Point Companion*** are:
- **2** [[Companions#Companion Skill List|Companion Skills]] at **+3**.
- If it is trained to [[SoC • Companion's Skill - Attack|Attack]], and it hits, it deals **1d4** damage.
- **7** [[Armor Class (AC)|AC]].
- [[Enemy Primer|HP]]: **7**.
