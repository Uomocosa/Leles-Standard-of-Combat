_Learn the art of the **Forge** to_:
- [[Custom Equipment|Craft]] and [[Heal and Repair|Repair]] [[Equipment List|Equipment]].
- Identify and understand how a certain [[Custom Equipment|Gear]] works.

You can [[Custom Equipment#Create a New Equipment|Craft]] or [[Custom Equipment#Upgrade a New Equipment|Upgrade]] a piece of Equipment, by spending **1 hour** to work on it, and then make a [[Forging]] [[Skill Check]] with a [[Skill Checks|DC]] of **10 + 3 times the total cost of the equipment**, if you succeed you can spend **Hero Points** to pay its cost and obtain the **equipment**. 

You can also work **1 hour** on a piece of [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]] Equipment to [[Heal and Repair|Repair]] it. To succeed make a [[Forging]] [[Skill Check]] with a [[Skill Checks|DC]] of **5 + 3 times the total cost of the equipment**.

You can try to [[Custom Equipment#Create a New Equipment|Craft]], [[Custom Equipment#Upgrade a New Equipment|Upgrade]] or [[Heal and Repair|Repair]] a piece of Equipment this way, as part of a [[Rest and Recovery|short rest]].
