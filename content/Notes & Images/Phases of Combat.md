### Combat
1. ***Declaring Intentions***.<br>At the start of each [[#One Round|Round]], all creatures involved in combat must choose **2 actions** and declare them to the **GM**. These represent your intentions for the round, what you plan to do.
	- _If you are using the **Card System**_ — Place **2 cards** from your [[Active Deck]] face-down. These can include [[Type of Cards|Action Cards]], [[Type of Cards|Reaction Cards]], [[Type of Cards|Gear Cards]], or any other card type, as long as it makes sense for the situation. *For example, if you have an item in hand, you may set its card face down to indicate you intend to use it*.
2. ***Initiative***.<br>The **GM** decides the turn order, whether enemies or players act first.<br>The initiative may change after every round, or remain the same, depending on the situation.
3. ***Enemy Turn***.<br>During the enemies’ turn:
	- You can only use **Reactions** (or [[Type of Cards|Reaction Cards]]) in response to enemy actions.
	- If the **GM** grants you an [[#Fleeing from Danger / Opprtunity Attacks|Opprtunity Attacks]], you may choose one of your declared actions (or set cards) that you haven’t used yet and perform it as a **Reaction**.
4. ***Player Turn***.<br>During the players’ turn:
	- Decide with your allies which one of you goes first.
	- Perform the actions you declared at the start of the round.
	- You may choose to forgo one or more of your actions.
	- _If you are using the **Card System**_ — Turn face-up the cards you set face-down to reveal and use them. If you decide not to use a card, you may return it to your [[Active Deck]].

### Surprise Round
_The party of adventurers cautiously approaches a narrow forest path. Thorny brambles obscure their vision, and the chirping of birds masks the sound of movement. Suddenly, the ranger halts and signals silence. Before the group can react, a band of goblins bursts from the underbrush, arrows nocked and ready._
_**DM:** "The goblins have successfully ambushed you. Everyone roll for initiative. Those of you who failed the Perception check are surprised and cannot act during this round."_

A **Surprise Round** occurs when one or more creatures are caught off guard at the start of combat. It represents a tactical advantage gained through stealth, deception, or ambush.

Usually it goes somenthing like this:
1. A creature or group must remain hidden or unnoticed by their targets. This often requires a successful [[Stealth]] [[Skill Check|check]].
2. The targets make [[Perception]] [[Skill Check|check]] to detect the ambush. If they fail to notice the threat, they are surprised.
3. If one or more creature succeds in being unnoticed, then they will act in an additional round before combat begins (the **Surprise Round**), afterwards combat goes as usual.

----
### One Round
After all creatures in combat have performed their action/actions one round has passed.
On average, and for reference one round lasts **6 seconds**.

### Fleeing from Danger / Opprtunity Attacks
*The innocent town guard realized that if the battle against the infamous **Lord Edgelordius** continued, he might never see the beautiful face of his husband again. Desperation filled his heart. Before attempting to escape, he let out a ferocious scream—a primal cry that briefly stunned the **Lord**. It was just enough to create an opening, forcing the **Lord** to step back. That single moment widened the distance between them, placing the guard slightly outside the deadly reach of **Lord's Edge-Sword's edge**. Seizing this chance, the guard turned and ran with all his might, his feet pounding the ground as he fled, never daring to look back*.

*If you are facing a one or more enemies alone, without any ally that can cover you and watch your back, you cannot easily flee from them.
Whenever you turn your back to an enemy you are giving them a change to react.
Whenever you try to distance yourself while in range of a melee wepon, you give the enemy an opportunity.
....*

Whenever you move, or perform a slow action, or similar your **GM** might say that "***it provokes opportunity attacks***", meaning that one or more enemies can use **1** of their actions as if they were [[Type of Cards|Reactions]] againts you.
You can mitigate this by trying to scare, distract, faint an attack or more generarly roll a [[Skill Check]] to gain a brief moment, so that no **opportunity attacks are given**.

*If you try to flee while one or more enemies are constantly watching you, if you are surrounded by enemies, or others, rember to set the [[Ability Card]] and a [[Type of Cards|Movement Card]] face down, to try and gain that brief distraction and then run, so that no opportunity attacks are given*.
