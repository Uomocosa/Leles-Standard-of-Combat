In this subdeck you'll put all the cards that represent your **character's state**, so:
- **Equiped** [[Type of Cards|Gear Cards]].
- [[Type of Cards|Health Cards]].
- Active [[Custom Spells]] that were cast on you and are still ongoing.
- Active [[Type of Cards|Condition Cards]].
- Any additional cards that represent your character, like a list of [[Skills|Skill Modifiers]], a drawing of your character, ...

This cards should be set on the table you are playing, so that your **GM** can clearely see them.
