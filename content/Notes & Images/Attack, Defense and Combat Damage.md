*Sustaining damage from hazards and attacks is part of any RPG. You’ll be hit by arrows, knock your head, sustain sword cuts, or drop items when exhausted*. 
*However your [[Custom Equipment|gear]] can help you mitigate the damage you take, by shielding you and taking the damage in your place*.

----
##### Attack Roll
An Attack Roll is usually an [[Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] made to hit an enemy, with a weapon, or effect of a spell. All creatures involved roll a **d20** and add a certain [[Skill Checks|skill modifier]] defined by the kind of **Attack**. If the **attacker** roll is **equal or higher** the attack is successful. 
Usually the skill modifier you use is either:
- [[Might|Might]] or [[Agility|Agility]] if you attack with a [[Equipment List|Weapon]]. 
- While if you cast a spell that requires an [[Opposing Rolls]], you'll roll on [[Arcane|Arcane]], [[Faith]] or [[Nature]].

If the attacker rolls a natural **20** on the attack roll, then it is considered a [[The Extremes|critical success]], the attack will hit even if the [[Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] is lost.
While a natural **1** is considered a [[The Extremes|critical failure]].

If both **attacker** and **defender**/**receiver** roll a **critical success** or **critical failure**, then both are treated as normal rolls.

Most weapon have a [[Damaged Gear|Damaged Condition]], meaning that if the **d20** result of the **attack roll** is very low (usually **2** or less), the weapon will become [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]], meaning it will loose all effects and it will only deal **1** damage, until [[Heal and Repair|Repaired]]

> *(Optional Rule) — Take a Risk*:<br>To speed up the combat flow, if a Creature attacks another with multiple **attacks**, roll all [[Opposing Rolls|opposing rolls]] together, and confront you highest one with theirs, then second highest and so on...<br>You have played [Risk](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_(gioco)) before you'll be familiar with this rule. ^optional-take-a-risk

##### Defensive Roll
If an **Attack** mentions an [[Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] then when you are on the receiving end of that **attack**, you'll make a **defensive roll** trying to evade it.

> *(Optional Rule) — 10 is All you Need*:<br>As a **GM** rolling all this **opposing rolls** can be quite tedious<br>You can decide to take **10** + [[Skill Checks|Skill]] modifier, as the [[Skill Checks|DC]] your players' [[Skill Check|Skill Checks]] need to overcome.<br>If you would roll with [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]]: **increase the DC by 3**.<br>If you would roll with **double** [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]]: **increase the DC by 5**.<br>If you would roll with **triple** [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]]: **increase the DC by 6**.<br>For [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] you need to **decrease** the [[Skill Checks|DC]] by the same values.^optional-take-a-risk

##### Damage Roll
Each attack has its own rules for how to calculate damage, and additional effects to take track of.
The most common way to deal damage is to throw a single [[damage dices|damage dice]], for example if you hit an enemy with a [[Equipment List|dagger]] you deal **1d4** damages.

----
##### Taking Damage
If you are successfully hit, you need to take damage.
[[Discard Pile|Discard]] from your [[State Deck]] (to your [[Discard Pile]]) a number of [[Health|Health Cards]] equal to the damage received.
