As a **GM** you can use **Zone Cards**, to define where the combat takes place.

If two cards touch each other, then a Creature can move between them.

To define the distance between two Creatures, count how many movements it would require to go from one Creature to the other.

You can also use a simple drawing instead of Zone Cards.
