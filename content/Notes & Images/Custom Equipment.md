_Gear is king for the life of an adventurer! It’s cheap, lying around, and often quite awesome in creative hands_.

[[Hero Points]] are at the base of this System. They can be seen as inspiration that allows you **Character** to grow, simple currency to [[Equipment List|Buy some Equipment]] or [[Basic Item List|Items]]. Or even special parts that allows you to build or modify one.
   
In this chapter we'll focus on **Custom Equipment**. As mentioned above, you can use [[Hero Points]] to create a brand new [[Basic Item List|Equipment]], or [[Custom Equipment|Upgrade]] an existing one.

----
### Create a New Equipment

You can choose to create a new equipment if you have the necessary components (_enough [[Hero Points]]_).
Choose one of the equipment from the [[Equipment List|Equipment List]] by spending **1 hour** to work on it, you then make a [[Forging]] [[Skill Check]] with a [[Skill Checks|DC]] of **10 + 3 times the cost of the equipment**, if you succeed you can spend **Hero Points** to pay its cost and obtain the **equipment**.

Failing the [[Forging]] [[Skill Check]] won't consume any [[Hero Points]].

Other members of your party could ask you to craft a piece of Equipment for them, to pay its cost you can ask them to give you the necessary **Hero Points**.

The [[Equipment List|Basic Equipment]] and [[Basic Item List|Basic Items]] lists, are to be taken as a reference, and **if your GM allows**, you can come up with completely new ones.

----
### Upgrade a New Equipment

Every [[Custom Spells|Effect]] that has a duration of **1 hour** or more, and costs **2** **pts** or less, can be chosen as an **Upgrade**.

**Upgrading** — Like a [[SoC • Spell Effect - Passive|Passive Effect]], The **Effect**/**s** chosen as an **Upgrade** will not end and cannot be [[SoC • Spell Effect - Dispel|Dispelled]]. However the **Effect**/**s** chosen as an **Upgrade** can be active on the **Creature** that holds the **Gear**, or on the **Gear** itself (_your choice_).
Also remember that: if the **Gear** is [[Damaged Gear|Damaged]] it will only deal **1** damage and it looses all **Effects**, until it is [[Heal and Repair|Repaired]]. (_meaning it will loose all Upgrades as well, until repaired_)

**1 Point Upgrade** — You can **upgrade your weapon** by paying **1 pt**, choose an [[Custom Spells|Effect]] that costs the same, and has duration of **1 hour** or more, and use it as an **Upgrade**.<br>If you want to Increase the **Upgrade** Effect or add another one see the [[#^2-point-upgrade|2 Point Upgrade]].

**2 Points Upgrade** — You can **upgrade your weapon** by paying **2 pts**, choose one [[Custom Spells|Effect]] that cost the same (and has duration of **1 hour** or more) or **2** Effects that cost **1 pts** each (and have duration of **1 hour** or more), and use it/them as an **Upgrade**.<br>However the **Gear** will also gain one [[#Gear Defects Lists|Gear Defect]] as well (_see the following list_). ^2-point-upgrade

If you have the necessary components ([[Hero Points]]), you can spend **1 hour** to work on a piece of **Gear**, you then make a [[Forging]] [[Skill Check]] with a [[Skill Checks|DC]] of **10 + 3 times the total cost of the equipment**, if you succeed you can spend the **points** to obtain the **equipment**.
(_E.g. If you want to upgrade your **1 pts** gear with a **2 pts upgrade** its total cost would be **3**, so the [[Forging]] **DC** would be **19**_.)

Failing the [[Forging]] [[Skill Check]] won't consume any [[Hero Points]].

> *(Optional Rule) — With What Forge?*<br>To **Create** and/or **Upgrade** a piece of **Gear**, you obviously first need to find a forge, usually in a village. ^optional-rule-with-what-forge

### Gear Defects Lists
1. ***[[SoC • Gear Defect - Breakable|Breakable]]*** — _A fragile piece of equipment that can easily break under pressure, especially when things go wrong_.
2. ***[[SoC • Gear Defect - Sought After|Sought after]]*** — _An item with a dark allure, attracting the attention of dangerous individuals with questionable intentions_.
3. ***[[SoC • Gear Defect - Specialized|Specialized]]*** — _Sacrifices an advantageous property for a more focused (but less versatile) function_.
4. ***[[SoC • Gear Defect - Wonky|Wonky]]*** — _A quirky piece of gear prone to unpredictable malfunctions, often leading to humorous or surprising outcomes_.
5. ***[[SoC • Gear Defect - Heavy|Heavy]]*** — _A cumbersome piece of equipment that requires great mastery to wield effectively_.
6. ***[[SoC • Gear Defect - Garish|Garish]]*** — _A garish and intimidating piece of gear that attracts unwanted attention, either provoking enemies or causing social discomfort_.
