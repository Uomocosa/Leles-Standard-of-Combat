Gear is king for the life of an adventurer! It’s cheap, lying around, and often quite awesome in creative hands. You can use hero points to acquire and upgrade equipment. 
Like all pointspending in the game, this can only be done while resting in a safe place with the aid of an expert... in this case, a smith, mentor, or artificer. 

Gear has a [[#Gear|base cost]], gets more expensive with [[#Gear Effects Lists|Effects]], and earns a discount with [[#Gear Limitation Lists|Limitations]]. This allows you to buy a super simple dagger, or craft a very specific magic item all with the same value system:
- Any given [[#Gear Effects Lists|Effect]] added to gear can only be applied **ONCE PER ITEM**, though an item may have multiple effects.
- Any given [[Custom Spells|Limitation]] added to gear can only be applied **ONCE PER ITEM**, though an item may have multiple limitations.
- You can only add a maximum of **18** points of [[Custom Spells|Effects]] to a single gear. (*see [[Total Effect Cost]]*)
- A piece of equipment cannot go below **1 pt cost**.
- Upgrading or Creating **Gear** can only be done at a smithy or artisan, *and if you task others to craft for you they might add a commission fee!*.<br>To successfully upgrade or create a gear yourself you need to pass a [[Forging]] [[Skill Check|check]] with [[DC]] equal to **10 + the total cost of the gear you wish to make**.<br>***You cannot [[The Extremes|critically succeed]] in this roll***, treat a **20** as you would with any other result.<br>Every roll you make will require you to spend **1 hour** by a forge, smithy or similar, if you fail the [[Skill Check]] you spend **1** hour with no results, but no [[Hero Points]] are lost.<br>*So the [[Skills|DC]] to make a simple dagger is **11***.<br>*However to create a perfect siege weapon (5 points) with 6 effects (additional 18 points) and no defects the [[DC]] will be **33***.

### Gear
- ***[[SoC - 1 Point Gear|1 Point Gear]]*** — _Small weapons, pouches, rope, lantern, torch, climbing kit, travel rations, staff, ring or jewelry item, cloak, boots_.
- ***[[SoC - 2 Point Gear|2 Point Gear]]*** — _Medium-sized weapons, light armor, flares, bear traps & snares, thieves’ tools, common supplies_.
- ***[[SoC - 3 Point Gear|3 Point Gear]]*** — _Heavy and exotic weapons, mechanized weapons and tools, gear for horses, complex or heavy armor_.
- ***[[SoC - 5 Point Gear|5 Point Gear]]*** —  _Firearms, siege weapons, war-ready equipment and armor_.

### Gear Effects Lists
(***3 pts** Cost each*)
1. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Balanced|Balanced]]*** — _A versatile weapon that adapts its damage based on whether you strike with precision or brute strength, enhancing your natural abilities_.
2. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Bleed|Bleed]]*** — _A weapon that inflicts deep wounds, causing the target to bleed and suffer additional damage over time_.
3. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Cold Forged|Cold Forged]]*** — _A durable and resilient piece of metal equipment, impervious to rust and far harder to break or damage_.
4. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Concealed|Concealed]]*** — _A discreet and practical piece of gear that blends seamlessly into the surroundings, avoiding notice and hindrance_.
5. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Chained|Chained]]*** — _A secure and unstealable piece of gear, impervious to theft or loss_.
6. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Deadly|Deadly]]*** — _A weapon that gains extra raw power, adding another layer of damage to each strike_.
7. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Upgraded|Upgraded]]*** — _A powerful enhancement that significantly increases the potency of your weapon's damage, making every strike devastating_.
8. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Lightweight|Lightweight]]*** — _A masterfully crafted piece of equipment that is lighter and more practical, removing some of its previous limitations_.
9. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Precise|Precise]]*** — _A weapon that enhances your striking power, making every attack more accurate.
10. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Lucky Weapon|Lucky weapon]]*** — _A weapon so finely crafted that even near-perfect hits are treated as critical strikes, ensuring maximum impact_.
11. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Plated|Plated]]*** — _A resilient piece of gear that adds extra protection, bolstering your health with each enhancement_.
12. ***[[SoC • Gear Effect - Reinforced|Reinforced]]*** — _A durable piece of gear that enhances your health reserves, making you tougher to harm with each upgrade_.

### Gear Limitation Lists
(*Gain **3 pts** for each*)
1. ***[[SoC • Gear Limitiation - Breakable|Breakable]]*** — _A fragile piece of equipment that can easily break under pressure, especially when things go wrong_.
2. ***[[SoC • Gear Limitiation - Sought After|Sought after]]*** — _An item with a dark allure, attracting the attention of dangerous individuals with questionable intentions_.
3. ***[[SoC • Gear Limitiation - Stolen|Stolen]]*** — _A cursed item that brings the wrath of authorities or dangerous individuals, ensuring pursuit and conflict_.
4. ***[[SoC • Gear Limitiation - Wonky|Wonky]]*** — _A quirky piece of gear prone to unpredictable malfunctions, often leading to humorous or surprising outcomes_.
5. ***[[SoC • Gear Limitiation - Heavy|Heavy]]*** — _A cumbersome piece of equipment that requires great strength to wield effectively_.
6. ***[[SoC • Gear Limitiation - Garish|Garish]]*** — _A garish and intimidating piece of gear that attracts unwanted attention, either provoking enemies or causing social discomfort_.
