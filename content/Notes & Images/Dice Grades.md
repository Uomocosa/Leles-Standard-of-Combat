> Dice grades go like this: 
> ***1*** ➜ ***d4*** ➜ ***d6*** ➜ ***d8*** ➜ ***d10*** ➜ ***d12***.
> The maximum dice grade is the **d12**.

***Examples***:
- An increase of **1** dice grade from **d4** goes to **d6**.
- An increase of **1** dice grade from **d6** goes to **d8**.
- An increase of **2** dice grade from **d4** goes to **d8**.
- An increase of **2** dice grade from **d6** goes to **d12**.
- An increase of **2** dice grade from **d10** only goes to **d12**.
