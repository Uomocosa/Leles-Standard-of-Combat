<br>**Concealed 1d6** — This Weapon is easy to hide. If you take an enemy by surprise, you can [[Attack, Defense and Combat Damage|Attack]] with this weapon by making an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|opposing roll]]: _[[Deception]] or [[Stealth]] **vs.** [[Perception]] (roll the highest)_, if you succeed you deal **1d6** damages, if you fail you'll still deal the normal weapon's damage. _Make it count, usually after making an attack most Creature will notice you!_
