> ***Advantage***: throw one more **d20**, and keep the **highest** result.
> ***Disadvantage***: throw one more **d20**, and keep the **lowest** result.

> If you have both an **Advantage** and a **Disavantage** on the same [[Making Rolls|roll]] then, thow a single **d20**.

> Mulitple **Advantages** can stack, if you have **double Advantage** on a roll: throw **3d20** and keep the **highest** result.
> The same is true for multiple **Disadvantages**.
