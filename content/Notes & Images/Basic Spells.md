----

**When using basic spells in play, answer almost every question with the [[Spells • The Defaults|defaults]]**. 
Casting [[SoC • Basic Spell - Healing Light|Healing Light]]? [[Healing and Repair|Heal]] **1** [[Health]] from a target you touch. 
Casting [[Mage’s Cloak]]? Touch the target cloak, and its wearer will be targeted last for **1** [[Phases of Combat|round]]. 
These default values should feel limiting quickly to the magic-user. That is when useful upgrades like Powerful, Lasting, or Dice Upgrade can be bought with hero points.

----
*Considering the [[Spells • The Defaults|defaults]], you’ll be feeling flimsy with touch-only spells at your most basic level. Always consider throwing the ‘[[SoC • Rune - Ranged|Ranged]]’ upgrade on basic spells right from the get-go for 3 points. Trust me on this one*.
