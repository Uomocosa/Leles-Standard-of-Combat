You can create a scroll or potion by imbuing a [[Custom Spells|Spell]] you know, that does not have a [[Custom Spells|Limitation]], and pay half its cost in [[Hero Points]] (_rounded up_).
You also need to spend **1 hour** imbuing the spell, and succeed an [[Arcane]], [[Nature]] or [[Faith]] [[Skill Checks|check]] with [[Skill Checks|DC]] equal to **10 + 3 times the cost of the spell***.

A spell casted from a scroll or potion requiring one or more [[Skill Checks|Skill Checks]] uses the [[Skill Checks|Modifiers]] of the Creature that made it.
After the spell imbued in a Scroll or Potion is casted the item is [[Destroyed Gear|Destroyed]].

An unknown scroll can be difficult to read, but since you made it you can easily teach it your allies, so that they can use it as well.
To decipher a scroll, you need to spend **1 hour** studying it, and succeed an [[Intuition]] [[Skill Checks|check]] with [[Skill Checks|DC]] equal to **10 + 3 times the cost of the spell***.
If you read the scroll out-loud you simply cast the spell.
You can also activate the scroll and lay it on a surface, when a Creature touches it, the [[Custom Spells|Spell]] gets casted.

A potion does not require any deciphering you can drink it, or throw it, and the spell effects are applied on the Creature or [[Combat Zones|Zone]] it falls on.<br>You can throw it in your [[Combat Zones|Zone]] or a nearby one without difficulty. If you wish to target a Creature with the potion (_up to **1** [[Combat Zones|Zone]] away_) you need to succeed in an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]]: _[[Agility]] vs. [[Agility]]_.
A potion is really difficult to decipher, you need to spend **1 hour** studying it, and succeed an [[Intuition]] [[Skill Checks|check]] with [[Skill Checks|DC]] equal to **10 + 5 times the cost of the spell***.
