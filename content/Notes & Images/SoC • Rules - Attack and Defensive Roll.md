- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>_Attack Roll_ — An **[[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]]** made to hit one or more Creatures, maybe with a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - BASIC EQUIPMENT|Weapon]], or effect of a [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SPELLS|Spell]]. All creatures involved roll a **d20** and add a certain [[SoC • Rules Chapter - SKILLS|Skill Modifier]] defined by the kind of **Attack**. If **a Defender's** result is **equal or higher** than **the Attackers's**: _the Attack hits him!_.<br><br>_Defensive Roll_ — When you **receive an Attack** that requires an **[[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]]**, you'll make a **Defensive Roll** to try and avoid it.<br><br>_The Extremes_ — If **the Attacker** rolls a **natural 20** on the attack roll, then it is considered a [[SoC • Rules - Critical Success and Failure|Critical Success]], the Attack will hit, and the Defender cannot roll his [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]]. Otherwise if **the Attacker** rolls a **natural 1** the Attack Fails!
