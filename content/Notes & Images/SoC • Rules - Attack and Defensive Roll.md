- ***Type***: Rules
- ***Description***:<br>_Defensive Roll_ — An Attack Roll is usually an [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] made to hit an enemy, with a weapon, or effect of a spell. All creatures involved roll a **d20** and add a certain [[Skill Checks|skill modifier]] defined by the kind of **Attack**. If the **attacker's** result is **equal or higher** than the **defender's**: _the attack is successful_.<br><br>_Defensive Roll_ — When you **receive an Attack** that requires an **Opposing Roll**, you'll make a **Defensive Roll** to try and dodge it.<br><br>_The Extremes_ — If the attacker rolls a natural **20** on the attack roll, then it is considered a [[The Extremes|critical success]], the attack will hit even if the [[SoC • Rules - Opposing Rolls|Opposing Roll]] is lost. While a natural **1** is considered a [[The Extremes|critical failure]]. If both **attacker** and **defender**/**receiver** roll a **critical success** or **critical failure**, then both are treated as normal rolls.
