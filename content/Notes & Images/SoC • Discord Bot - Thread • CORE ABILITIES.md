If you wish, you can select a *Core Ability* to gain a mechanical specialization.
- **A character can have at most one**. 
- Core abilities cost **7 hero points** (_unless specified otherwise_).
# Core Ability List
1. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Artificer|Artificer]]*** — _A master artisan who enhances magic through crafted items, imbuing spells with greater potency_.
2. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Alchemist|Alchemist]]*** — _They unlock the hidden potential of the mundane, creating fleeting brilliance with bubbling brews_.
3. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Battlemaster|Battlemaster]]*** — _A swift and precise combatant, master of melee-fights_.
4. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Brutal Fighter|Brutal Fighter]]*** — _A battle-hardened warrior who strikes with overwhelming force and relentless power_.
5. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Druid|Druid]]*** — _A skilled survivalist who thrives in the wild, adapting to nature and transforming at will._.
6. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Detective|Detective]]*** — _An intuitive mastermind who uncovers hidden truths and aids allies with precise foresight_.
7. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Go Unnoticed|Go Unnoticed]]*** — _A stealthy trickster who easily vanishes into shadows_.
8. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Hero|Hero]]*** — _A skilled spellcaster who imbues magic with enduring power, making effects last longer with ease_.
9. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Lucky|Lucky]]*** — _A being of uncanny fortune, whose luck always seems to tip the scales in critical moments_.
10. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Master of the Forge|Master of the Forge]]*** — _A versatile craftsman who imbues gear with adaptable enhancements_.
11. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Paragon of Faith|Paragon of Faith]]*** — _A devout spellcaster whose faith grows stronger with every cast, purging weak undead with divine might_.
12. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Runic Caster|Runic Caster]]*** — _A spellweaver who crafts tailored magic through inscribed runes, adapting their power to fit every need_.
13. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Spellsinger|Spellsinger]]*** — _A charismatic spellcaster who weaves magic through words and presence, captivating those who listen_.
14. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Uncanny Shot|Uncanny Shot]]*** — _A sharp-eyed marksman with quick reflexes, striking with deadly accuracy even in the most precise shots_.
15. ***[[SoC • Core Ability - Wizard Savant|Wizard Savant]]*** — _A vessel of raw magical energy, channeling power with the potential for overwhelming force_.
