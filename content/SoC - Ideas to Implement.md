##### Overhaul the System
*Inspired by [Crow and Skull](https://www.runehammer.online/crown-skull-rpg), and [Balatro](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/Balatro/)*
- Use a deck of normal playing cards as a minigame for combat, these cards help you describe the scene.
- All "created" Cards refer to these cards.<br>For example:
	- "***Any 1 Card*** — deal 1 damage to a target of your choosing".
	- "***Each Black Card*** — deal 1 damage to a target of your choosing".
	- "***Each Diamond*** — Create a wall with 15 HP between two Zones of your choosing".
	- "***Jack of Spades*** — Create a minion with 15HP, a the end of all of your turns it deals **1d4** damage to one target".
	- "***Ace of Hearts*** — Heal one Creature up to 10 HP".
- The effect is created as usual, however the card/s that triggers the effect decides how much points you can invest in this effect.<br>Limitation work as usual.
- Zones have similar effects each turn
